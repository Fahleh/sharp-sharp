Take the order. Beat the go-slow. Ride sharp sharp.

How to play

  • Goal: pick up each order, deliver it before the tip runs out. 5 minutes, one bike.
  • Keys: arrows or WASD to ride, Space to brake, P to pause.
  • Touch: the bike rides itself. Steer with the arrows, hold the brake to slow down.
